F14 n_pax_size_disagrees: rejected

A pax `size` that disagrees with the header's size, which hides a header from one implementation.

The header of `a` says size 512, and its pax extended header says `size=0`. An implementation that
takes the pax size reads the next block as the header of another file, `smuggled`; an implementation
that takes the header's size, or does not know pax, reads that block as the data of `a`.

Decision point: which of two sizes frames the member.
Seen in: no tarball of the scans.
