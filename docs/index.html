tar-minefield

A corpus of tar archives with verdicts, for strict parsers, which reject what parsers can disagree on, and what 20 parsers make of each. Every case has a code: its letter is the level of the catalogue of METHOD.md, F framing, N names, K kinds, S order and state, G global pax headers. A code stays with its case; new cases get new codes. See the repository for the method and the scans. The explanations of the cases are written by hand; the numbers, outcomes, block maps and dumps are generated from the archives, the scans and the runs of the parsers.

The cases

Prevalence: the share of the 62622 distinct tarballs of the scans that have the feature of the case; none, if no tarball has it. A dash: the scanner has no feature for the case. The page of a case has the numbers per ecosystem.
