F04 end-missing

No end-of-archive marker: the archive ends after a complete member.

Verdict
tolerated: the default policy accepts it, and a strict parser may reject it
Level
framing
Default policy
accepts end.missing: the archive ends after a member, and its sha256 is pinned

The case

The default policy accepts it: the archive is pinned by its sha256, so one that ends early is caught before it is parsed.

Seen in: SDL2_image 2.8.8, ruby 4.0.5 and fastcap 2.0-18Sep92.
