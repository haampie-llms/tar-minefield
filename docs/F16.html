The case

POSIX says a reader treats an unknown typeflag as a regular file, so its size is data. A reader that skips members it does not know may skip only the header, and then read the data as the next header, here that of smuggled.

Decision point: whether an unknown typeflag has data.

Seen in: no tarball of the scans.
