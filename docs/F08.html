F08 end-truncated-in-member

An archive that ends inside the data of a member.

Verdict
to reject: implementations disagree on its tree
Level
framing
Default policy
rejects it

The case

The header of a says 1024 bytes, but the file ends after 512 of them. An implementation may extract the part it has, extract nothing, or fail. Archives that are distributed are pinned by their sha256, so this archive is damaged, not ambiguous: it is rejected all the same.

Decision point: whether a member that is cut short is extracted.

Seen in: no source tarball, where a damaged download fails its sha256.

Every block of 512 bytes, to scale, with its offset. Hover over a field for its bytes.
