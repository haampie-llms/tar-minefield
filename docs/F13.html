F13 pax-newline-in-path

A newline in a pax path, where readers that split records on lines see another record.

Verdict
rejected: readers disagree on its tree
Level
framing
Default policy
rejects it
Files
the script, the archive, every block as text

The case

pax records are delimited by their length, so a value may hold a newline. A reader that splits the extended header on newlines instead sees the record end at it, and the rest as a record of its own: here path=evil. The path is good\n1 path=evil to one reader, good or evil to others.

Decision point: whether a pax record is delimited by its length or by a newline.

Seen in: newlines in pax values only in xattrs, such as Minion 10.31, which the default policy accepts as xattrs are not extracted.
