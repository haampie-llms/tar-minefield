F09 end-zero-block-then-member

A zero block, then another member: the end of the archive, or not?

Verdict
rejected: readers disagree on its tree
Level
framing
Default policy
rejects it
Files
the script, the archive, every block as text

The case

POSIX ends an archive with two zero blocks, and GNU tar stops at the first zero block it reads by default, while --ignore-zeros reads on. After one zero block comes a file, hidden, which some readers extract and others never see.

Decision point: whether one zero block ends the archive.

Seen in: jakarta-oro 2.0.8, PullParser 2.1.10 and commons-cli 1.2-src.
