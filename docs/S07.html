S07 tree-duplicate-file-differs

The same file twice, with different contents: the first copy, or the last?

Verdict
rejected: readers disagree on its tree
Level
order and state
Default policy
rejects it
Files
the script, the archive, every block as text

The case

a is written twice. Extractors that write members in order keep the last copy; readers that look a name up in an index, or that refuse to overwrite, keep the first. The two trees differ in the contents of a.

Decision point: which of two members of one path is the file.

Seen in: fish 4.7.1, where Cargo.toml and Cargo.lock are appended again without their last newline, and logstash 8.15.2, where build.rb is first a license header and last the build's date and commit. Most tarballs with duplicates, mostly from cabal sdist, have identical copies: the default policy rejects those too, as a reader would have to compare the copies to tell.
